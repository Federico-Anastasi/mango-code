First release. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Code_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have one of the pre-releases, the app offers this version by itself at the next start, and it installs over it keeping your conversations and models. At first run the app looks at your graphics cards, processor and memory, proposes the models that fit, and downloads the matching engine and the models you pick.

**Changed since rc.4**
- Mango Code on your phone is a web app made for the phone: one top bar with back, the conversation title, new conversation, changes and a menu; conversation text at 16 px; the prompt always at the bottom, also with the keyboard open; big buttons. It can be added to the home screen.
- From your phone you can see the page the computer's browser panel is showing, through the same login as the rest of the app, and open or download the documents of the conversation's project (web pages, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images).
- Home on the phone now shows the projects and conversations of the computer, so you can pick up where you left off.
- The phone's entry page explains how to get in, and the app's pages are never kept stale by the phone's cache.
- Files outside your projects are no longer treated as inside one.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a Windows certificate: SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- The app is tuned only for one set of cards (RTX 3060 Ti + GTX 1080 + GTX 1660 SUPER); on other hardware it uses a general plan that works but is not optimized.
- Small models (Qwen3.5-2B) can misread a short first message and answer something unrelated: ask again.
- Qwen3.8-27B-TOQ-v1 needs the `toq` engine, downloaded automatically. As an agent it is less reliable than Qwen3.6.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with your graphics cards, processor and memory and what happened.
