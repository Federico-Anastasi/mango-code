Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Code_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have 0.1.0, the app offers this version by itself at the next start, and it installs over it keeping your conversations and models.

**Changed since 0.1.0**
- Fixed: 0.1.0 closed right after loading on most processors (all those without AVX-512). If 0.1.0 never opened on your PC, install this version by hand with the installer below: the automatic update needs an app that starts.
- Use the engine of another PC: on the PC with the graphics cards turn on "Share" and copy the `mango://` code; on the other PC choose "Use" and paste it. The model runs on the first PC, the conversation stays on yours.
- Before you download a model, the app tells you roughly how fast it will be on your PC.
- PDFs and images open in the preview panel, next to the conversation.
- You can start a conversation without choosing a project folder.
- Desktop, Documents and other broad folders can no longer be a project: the app creates a subfolder.
- Dictation is your choice: the app tells you how much space it takes and downloads it only if you want it.
- Uninstalling removes models, engines and logs and keeps your conversations and settings.
- Mango Code on your phone listens only on the networks shown in the settings (home network and Tailscale).
- Downloads go through Windows' proxy and certificates, and the app says clearly when a company filter blocks one.
- When the agent cannot find the text to edit, it is shown the closest lines, so it gets the edit right sooner.
- Settings are shorter: the options that did nothing are gone, and the Team button shows in the chat only when Team is on.
- The terminal version is removed; `mango-launch.exe -p` stays as the way to use Mango Code from a script.
- The agent is based on opencode 1.19.5, with two security fixes from upstream.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a Windows certificate: SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- The app is tuned only for one set of cards (RTX 3060 Ti + GTX 1080 + GTX 1660 SUPER); on other hardware it uses a general plan that works but is not optimized.
- Small models (Qwen3.5-2B) can misread a short first message and answer something unrelated: ask again.
- Qwen3.8-27B-TOQ-v1 needs the `toq` engine, downloaded automatically. As an agent it is less reliable than Qwen3.6.

**Engines in this release** (unchanged since 0.1.0)

- [mango-prism](https://github.com/Federico-Anastasi/mango-prism/releases/tag/prism-b10709-9a9394a) `prism-b10709-9a9394a`

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with your graphics cards, processor and memory and what happened.
