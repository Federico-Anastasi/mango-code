Fourth public pre-release. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Code_0.1.0-rc.4_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. It installs over the
earlier pre-releases and keeps your conversations and models. Pre-releases are not offered by the automatic update:
install this one by hand.

**Changed since rc.3**
- Zoom the window with Ctrl+ and Ctrl- (Ctrl+0 goes back to 100%); the zoom is remembered at the next start.
- The browser panel sits next to the chat from 768 px wide, also on laptops with display scaling, and steps aside while
  a dialog, menu or list is open above it (Settings no longer end up under the page).
- From your phone: Home shows the projects and conversations of the computer, so you can pick up where you left off;
  the app fills exactly the visible screen, with the keyboard open too; the logo and the tab titles fit a small screen.
- "Report a problem" can carry your own files (images, documents, anything up to 30 MB in all), chosen or pasted, as
  they are; the logs are still added by themselves. It also works from the phone.
- Programs and pages opened by the app stay open when the app quits.
- A new message no longer makes the engine re-read the previous turn: answers in a long conversation start sooner.
- The model knows what Mango Code is and what it can do, and answers from that instead of searching.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a Windows certificate: SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run
  anyway").
- The app is tuned only for one set of cards (RTX 3060 Ti + GTX 1080 + GTX 1660 SUPER); on other hardware it uses a
  general plan that works but is not optimized.
- Small models (Qwen3.5-2B) can misread a short first message and answer something unrelated: ask again.
